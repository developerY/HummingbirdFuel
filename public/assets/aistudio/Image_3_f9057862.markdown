## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-performance biohacking, cognitive acceleration, and circadian rhythm optimization. Rooted in the concept of precise neuro-metabolic energy delivery ("Artificial Intelligent Microdose Energy Spray — Energy All Day / Sleep All Night"), the interface evokes extreme focus, computational precision, and vital energy.

The aesthetic fuses **Futuristic Dark Minimalist** architecture with **Glassmorphism** and controlled **Kinetic High-Contrast Accents**. The base environment is a deep, immersive obsidian void that eliminates visual fatigue and sets an elite stage. Surfaces utilize tinted slate glass layers with precise micro-borders. High-energy thermogenic amber and neon-flame gradients puncture the darkness, drawing sharp visual hierarchy toward metabolic metrics, microdose spray toggles, and conversion funnels. Technical monospace elements convey formulaic precision, scientific credibility, and computational micro-dosing dosage controls.

## Colors

The color structure is engineered around contrast and luminous hierarchy on deep canvases:

- **Primary (`#ff5a1f`) & Secondary (`#ff8a00`)**: The core kinetic energy spectrum. Used for primary conversion triggers, active microdose states, flame gradient accents, and critical kinetic telemetry.
- **Tertiary (`#ffb347`) & Gold Spectrum (`#e2b338`)**: Radiance markers, circadian daytime markers, premium edition indicators, and active flame highlights.
- **Neutral Base (`#0a0a0c`)**: Absolute obsidian floor. Absorbs ambient light and grounds floating glass cards without harsh pure-black clip.
- **Slate Surfaces (`#121318` & `#1a1c24`)**: Translucent surface containers with cool-slate undertones providing separation from the canvas.
- **Electric Cyan (`#00f0ff`)**: Infrequently deployed auxiliary accent strictly for biological diagnostics, night/sleep circadian state shifts, and AI microdose calibration feedback.

## Typography

Typography delivers a precise balance of scientific authority and futuristic lifestyle energy:

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its razor-sharp geometric cuts, wide stance, and high-tech pedigree reminiscent of clean laboratory interfaces and aerospace engineering. Headline tracking is tightly pulled to create impactful display units.
- **Body & Editorial (Manrope)**: Delivers exceptional legibility on dark glass surfaces. Its modern neo-grotesque proportions balance high x-height with gentle humanistic terminals, ensuring fatigue-free reading across dosage instructions and scientific breakdowns.
- **Technical & Metric Accents (JetBrains Mono)**: Encodes AI microdose intervals, batch data, active ingredient ratios, and dosage counters with cold, unyielding mathematical certainty. Always applied to uppercase data tags and UI metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a responsive 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (`1280px` max-width content container) collapsing to a 6-column grid on tablet (`768px`) and a 4-column system on mobile devices (`375px+`).

- **Grid Alignment**: Sections maintain breathable rhythm with vertical section spacing scaling from `space-2xl` on mobile devices to `space-3xl` on desktop views.
- **Component Padding**: Interactive cards and control panels leverage a consistent `space-lg` inner breathing area, keeping critical information anchored safely away from illuminated borders.
- **Rhythm & Proximity**: Dense metric readouts (e.g., microdose sprays remaining, bioavailability absorption curves) utilize tight `space-xs` and `space-sm` gaps, while primary thematic sections maintain spacious cinematic distance.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is achieved through light emission, translucent layering, and chromatic edge highlights rather than traditional drop shadows:

1. **Surface 0 (Canvas Void)**: `#0a0a0c` solid foundation. Ambient background elements may feature faint radial glows (`rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.04)`) with 120px blur radii to indicate energy nodes.
2. **Surface 1 (Base Glass Deck)**: `#121318` rendered at 80% opacity with `16px` backdrop-filter blur. Outlined by a crisp 1px ghost border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Surface 2 (Elevated Kinetic Modules)**: `#1a1c24` at 85% opacity with `24px` backdrop-filter blur. Uses directional linear gradient borders that transition from `rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.4)` at the top edge to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` at the bottom edge.
4. **Ember Glow Elevation**: High-focus active states (hovered cards, primary purchase pods) project an external ambient luminescence: `0 0 32px rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.25), 0 0 1px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on controlled rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`, `8px` baseline). This choice prevents the clinical austerity of sharp corners while preserving high-tech precision.

- **Primary Interactive Elements**: Buttons, dosage toggles, and input fields use `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) or complete `pill` styling (for micro-tags, dynamic spray triggers, and status capsules).
- **Glassmorphic Feature Panels**: Utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to create sleek handheld hardware-like frames.
- **Inner Data Cells**: Use `rounded-sm` (0.25rem / 4px) to preserve internal structural density within parent cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button**: High-gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #ff5a1f 0%, #ff8a00 100%)`), uppercase Space Grotesk medium font, `label-lg` tracking, pure dark `#0a0a0c` text for extreme contrast. Hover triggers a radiant energy halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Glass Button**: Translucent slate background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), crisp 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), white text. On hover, the border shifts to `#ff8a00` with an amber text reflection.
- **Telemetry Micro-Trigger**: Pill-shaped button housing microdose spray counter buttons with embedded mono labels.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- Monospace metadata indicators (`label-sm`) with dark slate backgrounds, 1px translucent borders, and a pulsing ember dot (`#ff5a1f`) representing active bio-availability modes. Day formulas utilize amber badges; nocturnal recovery states adopt soft cyan or deep gold tokens.

### Product Showcase Cards
- Obsidian glass container featuring high-contrast rim lighting. Includes high-resolution bottle rendering with an offset ambient radial glow matching the bottle's formula flame. Integrated dosage calculator and live delivery state chips line the bottom margin.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: `#121318` background with an inset ghost border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). Focused state illuminates the perimeter with a 1px `#ff5a1f` stroke and subtle `0 0 12px rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.2)` inner glow. Typography set in JetBrains Mono for exact numerical or code entry.
- **Bio-Sliders & Steppers**: Custom energy dial/slider components filled with kinetic flame gradient tracks, displaying real-time microdose spray volume and estimated kinetic hours remaining.
